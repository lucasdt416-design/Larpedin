Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

LarpedIn is a parody website made for a hackathon. It is not affiliated with LinkedIn. This page describes what data the site uses.

What you type

When you generate a post, profile, battle, job list or delarp, the site sends your text to the Groq API to write the reply. Posts that you generate appear in the public Hall of Larp gallery on this site, together with the name that you entered. Do not type private information.

Where data is kept

The site has no database and no user accounts. Posts, profiles and votes are kept in server memory only, and they are deleted when the server restarts. Share links contain the shared post itself inside the link. Your browser stores your display name and your votes in local storage on your device.

Sign in with LinkedIn

If you choose to sign in with LinkedIn, the site receives an access token, your LinkedIn member ID and your name. They are used only to post the text that you confirm, to your own LinkedIn account. They are kept in server memory for at most 24 hours, and they are deleted when you sign out or when the server restarts. The site never posts without your click on "Post publicly", and it does not read your feed, connections or messages.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. We use no ads and no analytics trackers. The site is hosted on Render, and Render may keep standard server logs.
